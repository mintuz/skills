# Writing Formatting Guidelines

## Titles & H1

- Title case; 5–8 words (~50–60 chars) with one natural keyword.
- Make the benefit obvious (e.g., "X for Faster Y").
- Exactly one `<h1>` per page, containing the title.

## Headings & Structure

- `<h2>` for main sections, `<h3>` for subsections; avoid deeper levels.
- Add a heading roughly every 200–250 words; include the keyword once in a relevant `<h2>` and `<h3>`.
- Preserve logical flow; each heading should summarise its section's payoff.

## Paragraphs & Formatting

- Paragraphs of 3–5 sentences/lines. Lead with the point, then details.
- Bold sparingly for the primary keyword and key phrases; italics for light emphasis only.

## Lists & Consistency

- Move dense info and long inline enumerations into lists: bullets for unordered ideas, numbered lists for steps (when order matters) and rankings.
- Keep item phrasing consistent; end all items with a period or none.

## Intros & Outros

- Intro: 1–2 short paragraphs stating the goal and payoff. For tutorials, "In this guide you'll learn to…" plus what the reader needs before the first step (tools, access, running services) when the source facts establish it; do not invent prerequisites.
- Outro: 1–3 short paragraphs (optionally a brief bullet list) with key takeaways and a clear call-to-action.
