# Subtract Before You Add

Identify the behavior and guarantees the change must preserve. Check whether an existing mechanism can satisfy the new requirement before adding another owner, queue, validator or abstraction.

Remove demonstrated dead paths, duplicated rules and content-free references within scope; repair their maintained callers and links. Sequence independent safe subtraction before construction. Keep removal coupled to replacement when separating them would break the contract.

Two similar guards may protect different boundaries or mutations. Prove redundancy before deleting either; preserve failure behavior and its tests. Prefer observed requirements over speculative scaffolding.

Verify the affected public behavior and references after each coherent change. Judge the complete system, including shifted complexity, rather than celebrating fewer local lines. Stop when the accepted outcome is met on the simpler base.
