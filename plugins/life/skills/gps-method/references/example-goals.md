# GPS Example Goals

Load these examples only when the user asks for inspiration, a field remains abstract, or a quality benchmark is needed. The numbers and domain choices are illustrative; verify the user's real constraints and any specialist advice separately.

## Learning — ship production TypeScript work

- **Goal:** Ship two production-used TypeScript tools and pass an advanced assessment within 16 weeks.
- **Why:** Become the team's dependable reviewer for type-safe design and qualify for broader technical ownership.
- **Anti-goals:** Protect eight hours of sleep, use free learning material, and learn through original work rather than tutorial copies.
- **Major moves:** Study one chapter weekly; refactor one existing JavaScript tool; build one typed API client; complete weekly practice problems.
- **Confidence:** Theory 85%; practice 80%.
- **Failure forecast:** difficult concepts stall progress → ask a senior engineer after a two-hour attempt; projects expand → time-box each to four weeks; work displaces study → reserve three recurring morning blocks.
- **System:** Track chapters, project milestones, and assessment scores in one page; use calendar blocks; demo progress to a senior engineer every two weeks.

## Relationship — restore consistent connection

- **Goal:** Complete one phone-free date and five 15-minute check-ins each week for 12 weeks, then jointly review relationship satisfaction.
- **Why:** Rebuild connection before distance becomes the default.
- **Anti-goals:** Keep spending within the normal entertainment budget, share the emotional work, and address recurring conflicts directly.
- **Major moves:** Alternate planning weekly dates; hold short weekday check-ins; use one agreed structure for difficult conversations; review the relationship monthly.
- **Confidence:** Theory 85%; practice 75%, so simplify weekday check-ins to ten minutes and pre-book dates before proceeding.
- **Failure forecast:** fatigue cancels dates → keep a home-date fallback; check-ins feel forced → use one curiosity prompt; conflict is avoided → schedule the monthly review in advance.
- **System:** Use a shared calendar and monthly scorecard; set an evening cue; review follow-through together each Sunday.

## Creative — publish a short podcast season

- **Goal:** Publish a six-episode podcast season about independent software development within 12 weeks.
- **Why:** Share a decade of practical lessons and test whether podcasting is a satisfying creative medium.
- **Anti-goals:** Keep equipment spending below the agreed budget, preserve the creator's own voice, and let the first season remain a test.
- **Major moves:** Define six topics; record a pilot; batch the remaining episodes; publish every other week; share each episode in two relevant communities.
- **Confidence:** Theory 80%; practice 85%.
- **Failure forecast:** audio feels weak → test one modest microphone and editing preset; topics run dry → outline all six before launch; polishing delays release → cap editing at two hours per episode.
- **System:** Track episode status and listens in one sheet; reserve recording and publishing blocks; send each draft to a podcast peer before release.

## Financial — build an emergency buffer

- **Goal:** Build a separately held emergency buffer equal to the user's chosen number of essential-expense months by the agreed horizon.
- **Why:** Reduce anxiety and absorb genuine surprises without disrupting regular bills.
- **Anti-goals:** Preserve essential bills and a small leisure budget, define qualifying emergencies in writing, and keep the money in an appropriate low-risk account.
- **Major moves:** Calculate essential monthly expenses; automate a payday transfer; reduce selected discretionary costs; direct agreed windfalls to the buffer.
- **Confidence:** Theory 95%; practice 80%.
- **Failure forecast:** surprise costs interrupt saving → keep a checking cushion; withdrawals drift beyond emergencies → use the written definition and a waiting period; transfers become uncomfortable → review the amount monthly.
- **System:** Track the balance and projected completion date; automate transfers; hold a monthly review with the relevant household member.
