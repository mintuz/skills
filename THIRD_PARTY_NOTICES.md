# Third-party notices

Unless stated otherwise below, the skills listed below adapt Lauren Tan's [pstack](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/tree/dbf49813a39c1a5389c5e28cb8e4c1c2379e2454/pstack/skills) skills, pinned at commit [`dbf49813a39c1a5389c5e28cb8e4c1c2379e2454`](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/tree/dbf49813a39c1a5389c5e28cb8e4c1c2379e2454/pstack/skills).

This file contains the upstream MIT notices. The zip packager and the flat copier add it to each derived skill bundle so the notices travel with that copy. `design-handover` is an original skill and is not derived from pstack. So are `principle-define-done`, `principle-judge-with-fresh-eyes`, `principle-keep-secrets-out`, `principle-lead-with-the-answer`, `principle-preserve-meaning`, `principle-price-each-option` and `principle-report-every-transition`.

Adaptations narrow triggers, remove provider dependencies, preserve scoped authorization, and distinguish measured evidence from inference.

`adam-mode` and the `adam-agent` subagent are modelled on the pstack `poteto-mode` skill and `poteto-agent` subagent on the pstack `main` branch, which is later than the pinned commit.

`sharpen` adapts the pstack `sharpen` skill at commit [`eb4db2b55b2b7dec3d9282754cddf0a56bf4ff10`](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/tree/eb4db2b55b2b7dec3d9282754cddf0a56bf4ff10/pstack/skills/sharpen), which is later than the pinned commit. The skill does not exist at the pinned commit. The upstream `LICENSE` is unchanged at that commit.

`reframe` adapts the pstack `reframe` skill at commit [`9e278fe4d4ecd8bfe98b1b79bf4be662efb338cc`](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/tree/9e278fe4d4ecd8bfe98b1b79bf4be662efb338cc/pstack/skills/reframe), which is later than the pinned commit. The skill does not exist at the pinned commit. The upstream `LICENSE` is unchanged at that commit.

`writing-for-humans` includes material covered by the Lauren Tan, Peter Yang and Addy Osmani MIT notices below.

The formal-verification pair adapts the [pstack create and maintain workflows](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/tree/e58dd043600587dbaf58ba315e7f2b38373eeb43/pstack/skills) for the Everystate checker.

`architecture-audit`, `codebase-layout`, `hexagonal-architecture`, `twelve-factor`, `test-seams`, and the contract/dependency-check additions to `architect` adapt material from Paul Hammond's [citypaul/.dotfiles](https://github.com/citypaul/.dotfiles/tree/67d1b74409be1023016e2e6bc8731a33ce0ed4a9/claude/.claude/skills) skills, pinned at commit [`67d1b74409be1023016e2e6bc8731a33ce0ed4a9`](https://github.com/citypaul/.dotfiles/tree/67d1b74409be1023016e2e6bc8731a33ce0ed4a9/claude/.claude/skills). Within that set, `architecture-audit` and the `codebase-design` additions to `architect` also adapt Matt Pocock's [mattpocock/skills](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/tree/66898f60e8c744e269f8ce06c2b2b99ce7660d5f/skills/engineering) pinned at commit [`66898f60e8c744e269f8ce06c2b2b99ce7660d5f`](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/tree/66898f60e8c744e269f8ce06c2b2b99ce7660d5f/skills/engineering). Both upstream MIT notices appear below.
